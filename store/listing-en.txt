Adds trackpad gestures for opening this tab's history, closing a tab, and more.

🚀 Features
  ⭐️ Flick to go back or forward one step
  ⭐️ Keep pulling to open the list of pages this tab came through
  ⭐️ Or pull and hold still to open it (Chrome 151+)
  ⭐️ Choose from the list to jump several steps at once
  ⭐️ A tab with nothing to go back to just closes
  ⭐️ Turn it off on the sites where it gets in the way
  ⭐️ English, Korean, Japanese and Chinese

⚠️ Note
Chrome briefly shows a debugging notice bar when the list opens. Reading the back and forward order of the current tab is only possible through the debugger.
It does not work on chrome:// pages, the new tab page, or the Chrome Web Store.
Reload any tabs you had open before installing.
