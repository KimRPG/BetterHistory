A more capable back gesture for your trackpad.

🚀 Features
  ⭐️ Swipe and hold to see the list
  ⭐️ Scroll up and down the list to go back several steps at once
  ⭐️ Swiping on a tab with no history closes the tab

⚠️ Note
All features work properly only on Chrome 151 or later.
